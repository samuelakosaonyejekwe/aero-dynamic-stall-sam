What they are
-------------
Digitised oscillating-aerofoil load loops (C_L, C_M, C_D against incidence, plus
the same against time) for a NACA 0012, an AMES-01 and other sections. Each file
carries its own `authors` field, which reads "McAlister et al. (1982)".

Original measurements
  McCroskey, W.J., McAlister, K.W., Carr, L.W. & Pucci, S.L. (1982),
  "An Experimental Study of Dynamic Stall on Advanced Airfoil Sections",
  NASA TM-84245. A work of the U.S. Government.

Obtained via
  L. Pancini, "BL-DSM-JFS-2021", https://github.com/luizpancini/BL-DSM-JFS-2021 ,
  directory NASA Data/ . Checked on 9 September 2026: that repository states no
  licence, so no licence terms are asserted here on its behalf either. Anyone
  redistributing these files further should go back to those two sources.

How this study uses them
------------------------
06_postprocessing/validation/validate_nasa_real.py reads M, k, alpha_0,
delta_alpha, and the (alpha, C_L) and (alpha, C_M) point sets, and writes the
extracted points beside it as exp_<frame>_CL.csv / exp_<frame>_CM.csv. Those
extracts are derived from this same third-party data and carry the same caveat.

The airfoil identity of each frame is taken from the source repository's
src/functions/load_frame.m mapping: frames 7019-14220 = NACA 0012,
24022-31310 = AMES-01, >= 67000 = NLR-7301.
